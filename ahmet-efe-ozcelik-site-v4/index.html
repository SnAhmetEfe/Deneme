04 Use

Light that goes up is energy nobody uses.

At the end of the line, a lot of energy is spent on things nobody needs. City lighting is one of them.

Funded April 2025

TÜBİTAK 2209-A Research Project

Team Lead (project coordinator)

Research on Sustainable Recovery of Energy Losses Caused by Light Pollution in Cities. Streetlights and buildings send part of their light into the sky. That light is energy paid for and wasted. Our team of four is studying how much is lost and how it could be recovered.

W/01 · TÜBİTAK 2209-A · Team Lead · 2025

Energy lost to light pollution

Problem
Cities light the sky as well as the street. The light that escapes upward is wasted energy.
Approach
A funded student research project with a team of four, looking at how these losses could be measured and recovered.
Outcome
Selected for TÜBİTAK 2209-A funding. [add results when ready]
Learned

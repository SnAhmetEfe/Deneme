01 — What I believe

Three ideas that guide my work.

i.

The cleanest energy is the energy you never waste.

Before adding new capacity, it is worth asking how much is lost on the way. That question is behind my energy efficiency project.

ii.

Quality is a sustainability tool.

Every defect caught early is material, energy and time that do not have to be spent twice. I am seeing this every day in a factory quality team.

iii.

Nobody improves a system alone.

Leading IEEE teams taught me that good ideas need coordination, the right people and a clear plan to become real.

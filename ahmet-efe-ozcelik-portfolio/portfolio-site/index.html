About

Purpose before position

Learning the systems.
Improving what matters.

I’m an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at Manisa Celal Bayar University, building experience where energy, project leadership and responsible technology meet.

My path extends beyond the classroom. Leading teams within the IEEE MCBU Student Branch, coordinating multidisciplinary projects and working on a TÜBİTAK-supported energy-efficiency study have taught me to move from an idea to a structured, shared outcome.

I’m developing my career around five connected principles: sound engineering, reliable quality, continuous improvement, purposeful digitalization and credible sustainability. The goal is not to claim finished expertise—it is to build it deliberately, through technical work and measurable learning.

# Site architecture & content plan

## Positioning

**Brand idea:** Engineering a Sustainable Future  
**Desired perception:** An early-career engineer building a serious, long-term practice around quality, technology and sustainable industry.  
**Tone:** precise, curious, grounded, forward-looking. Never claim impact or expertise that the evidence cannot support.

## Source and authenticity rules

The published content was derived from the public LinkedIn profile supplied in the brief, accessed 6 November 2026:

- Headline, About, Experience, Education and Licenses & Certifications sections were used.
- Public profile facts are presented as profile statements, not independently audited claims.
- No private email or telephone number is included.
- No quality methodology, result metric, project tool or sustainability impact was invented.
- Unknown project methods/results are explicitly labeled “to be added” or “not publicly specified.”
- Quality, continuous improvement and digitalization appear as **development focus areas** where profile evidence is not yet sufficient to claim professional mastery.

## Information architecture

1. **Home / Hero** — name, verified current positioning, manifesto, LinkedIn CTA, procedural energy-system visual.
2. **About** — professional story connecting engineering education, project leadership and long-term career principles.
3. **Experience** — reverse-chronological timeline; the first seven roles are visible and three earlier roles expand on request.
4. **Engineering & Quality** — capability philosophy with clear “verified evidence” vs “development focus” language.
5. **Selected Projects** — two LinkedIn-supported project cases and one explicitly empty future case-study slot.
6. **Skills Ecosystem** — interactive network instead of proficiency bars; each node reveals its evidence status.
7. **Sustainability** — industrial → technology → renewable energy → nature story. The section explicitly avoids environmental performance claims.
8. **Education & Learning** — degree, preparatory year and six certifications with complete visible metadata.
9. **Contact** — LinkedIn only; no extracted or inferred private channel.

## Growth model

- Add a timeline `<article>` only after verifying company, role, dates and source wording.
- Duplicate a project card and fill all four fact fields: problem, contribution, methods/tools, impact/result.
- Replace the reserved project slot after the next verified case study.
- Convert “development focus” skill nodes to “profile-verified” only when evidence is published.
- Add a production domain to canonical/OG fields, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and JSON-LD before launch.
